## FunnyGuilds permissions

### `funnyguilds.*` — all FunnyGuilds permissions `OP`

`funnyguilds.admin` · `funnyguilds.vip` · `funnyguilds.player`

### `funnyguilds.admin` — permissions for the administrator `OP`

`funnyguilds.reload` · `funnyguilds.admin.build` · `funnyguilds.admin.interact` · `funnyguilds.admin.teleport` · `funnyguilds.admin.notification` · `funnyguilds.admin.disabledummy`

### `funnyguilds.vip` — permissions for VIP `OP`

`funnyguilds.vip.items` · `funnyguilds.vip.rank` · `funnyguilds.vip.base` · `funnyguilds.vip.baseTeleportTime`

### `funnyguilds.player` — basic permissions for the player `default`

`funnyguilds.ally` · `funnyguilds.base` · `funnyguilds.break` · `funnyguilds.buylife` · `funnyguilds.create` · `funnyguilds.delete` · `funnyguilds.deputy` · `funnyguilds.enlarge` · `funnyguilds.escape` · `funnyguilds.guild` · `funnyguilds.info` · `funnyguilds.invite` · `funnyguilds.items` · `funnyguilds.join` · `funnyguilds.kick` · `funnyguilds.leader` · `funnyguilds.leave` · `funnyguilds.panel` · `funnyguilds.playerinfo` · `funnyguilds.pvp` · `funnyguilds.rankreset` · `funnyguilds.statsreset` · `funnyguilds.setbase` · `funnyguilds.tnt` · `funnyguilds.validity` · `funnyguilds.war`

Opening `/panel` requires `funnyguilds.panel` and guild membership.
Panel actions also require the permissions of their corresponding commands.

In **Roles and permissions**, the guild leader can edit deputy and member
permissions, create custom roles, and set individual overrides. Bukkit nodes
such as `funnyguilds.pvp` are still required for the corresponding actions.

To let a deputy edit member permissions, enable **Manage permissions** for
them and set the **Delegation scope** in the deputy role view. Deputies can
change only the permissions they hold and are authorized to delegate. This
also applies to permissions inherited through a role they assign.

**View activity log** is a guild permission. The leader has it by default
and can grant it through a role or a player override.
