---
title: PlaceholderAPI
description: Exposing FunnyGuilds placeholders to other plugins through PlaceholderAPI.
---

Once [PlaceholderAPI](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/placeholderapi.6245/)
is detected, FunnyGuilds registers its own `%funnyguilds_x%` expansion — no
extra download via `/papi ecloud` needed. The expansion exposes the same
data as the [built-in placeholders](/en/placeholders/) (`{X}`), just in a
format other plugins (chat, GUI, scoreboards outside FunnyGuilds, etc.) can
read — the variable name after `funnyguilds_` corresponds directly to the
name from the built-in placeholder list.

## Player

- `%funnyguilds_player%`
- `%funnyguilds_ping%`
- `%funnyguilds_ping-format%`
- `%funnyguilds_points%`
- `%funnyguilds_points-format%`
- `%funnyguilds_kills%`
- `%funnyguilds_deaths%`
- `%funnyguilds_assists%`
- `%funnyguilds_logouts%`
- `%funnyguilds_kdr%`
- `%funnyguilds_position-<type>%`
- `%funnyguilds_guild-position%`
