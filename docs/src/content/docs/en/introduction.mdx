**FunnyGuilds** is a powerful, fully configurable RPG guild plugin for Paper-based
Minecraft servers. It lets players create guilds, fight over territory, form
alliances and compete in rankings — while giving admins hundreds of options to
tailor the experience to their server.

The project has been in continuous development since 2014 and remains free and
open source.

## Key features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Guilds & alliances">
		Create guilds, invite players, appoint deputies, form alliances and wage wars.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Guild territories">
		Protected regions with a guild heart, enlarging and TNT handling.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Rankings">
		Points, KDR/KDA and player & guild tops with multiple ranking types.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Tablist">
		A fully configurable tablist with animations and dozens of variables.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Placeholders">
		Hundreds of built-in placeholders plus [PlaceholderAPI](/en/placeholderapi/) integration.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Integrations">
		WorldEdit, WorldGuard, Vault, PlaceholderAPI, dynmap and DecentHolograms.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
