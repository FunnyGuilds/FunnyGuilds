Your relationship with another guild (neutral/ally/enemy) determines the
color of its tag in chat and the tablist (see [Chat](/en/chat/)) and whether
you can enter its territory (see
[Territories & protection](/en/territories/)).

## Alliances

`/sojusz <tag>` proposes an alliance to another guild — the other leader
accepts it with the same command. An existing alliance can be broken at any
time with `/rozwiaz <tag>`. A guild can have at most `max-allies` allies
(**15** by default).

By default allies can't damage each other — controlled by `damage-ally`. This
can also be overridden per-region with WorldGuard's `fg-friendly-fire` flag,
see [WorldGuard & WorldEdit](/en/worldguard-worldedit/).

## Wars

Attacking another guild (when `war-enabled` is on) requires declaring war on
it first with `/wojna <tag>` — unless `war-require-declaration` is disabled,
in which case any non-allied guild can be attacked without a declaration. A
guild can be at war with at most `max-enemies` guilds at once (**15** by
default).

| Option | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `war-protection` | 24h | Time after a guild is created during which it can't be attacked |
| `war-wait` | 24h | Cooldown between attacks on the same guild |
| `war-lives` | 3 | Number of guild lives |
| `war-heart-lives` | 1 | Heart hits needed to remove 1 life |
| `war-tnt-protection` | `true` | Whether the protection period also grants TNT immunity |

Destroying a guild's heart costs it one life; once all its lives are gone,
the guild loses the war. Setting `war-heart-lives` to `1` effectively
disables the heart's HP mechanic — every hit removes a life immediately.

Members of the same guild can't damage each other by default either — that is
controlled by `damage-guild`.

:::note[Other options]
`lives-repeating-symbol` sets the appearance of the `{G-LIVES-SYMBOL}`
placeholder — separate symbols for full and empty lives, and for any surplus
above `war-lives`.
:::

## Banning a guild

An admin can ban a guild with `/ga ban <tag> <time> <reason>` — for example
to temporarily exclude it from wars while moderating a dispute. The ban can
be lifted with `/ga unban <tag>`, but it also expires on its own after the
given time.
