Your relationship with another guild (neutral/ally/enemy) determines the
color of its tag in chat and the tablist (see [Chat](/en/chat/)) and whether
you can enter its territory (see
[Territories & protection](/en/territories/)).

## Alliances

`/sojusz <tag>` proposes an alliance to another guild — the other leader
accepts it with the same command. An existing alliance can be broken at any
time with `/rozwiaz <tag>`. A guild can have at most `max-allies` allies
(**15** by default).

By default allies can't damage each other — controlled by `damage-ally`. This
can also be overridden per-region with WorldGuard's `fg-friendly-fire` flag,
see [WorldGuard & WorldEdit](/en/worldguard-worldedit/).

## Wars

Attacking another guild (when `war.enabled` is on) requires declaring war on
it first with `/wojna <tag>` — unless `war.require-declaration` is disabled,
in which case any non-allied guild can be attacked without a declaration. A
guild can be at war with at most `max-enemies` guilds at once (**15** by
default).

### War modes (War Mode)

FunnyGuilds provides two war modes (`war.mode`):
- **`MODERN`** (default) — a dynamic war system where attackers can gain lives by conquering hearts (`conquer-gain-life`) and winning wars (`win-gain-life`), win streaks are tracked and announced, and anti-farming protection is enforced.
- **`LEGACY`** — classic war mode with no life regeneration or combat rewards.

| Option | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `war.mode` | `MODERN` | War system mode (`MODERN` or `LEGACY`) |
| `war.lives` | 3 | Base number of guild lives |
| `war.max-lives` | 5 | Maximum number of lives achievable via conquest or purchase |
| `war.heart-lives` | 1 | Heart hits needed to remove 1 life |
| `war.conquer-gain-life` | `true` | Whether the attacker gains +1 life on conquering a heart (MODERN mode) |
| `war.win-gain-life` | `true` | Whether winning a war grants +1 life to the winner (MODERN mode) |
| `war.protection` | 24h | Time after a guild is created during which it can't be attacked |
| `war.wait` | 24h | Cooldown between attacks on the same guild |
| `war.tnt-protection` | `true` | Whether the protection period also grants TNT immunity |

Destroying a guild's heart costs it one life; once all its lives are gone,
the guild loses the war and is deleted. Setting `war.heart-lives` to `1` effectively
disables the heart's HP mechanic — every hit removes a life immediately.

### Buying lives (`/g kupzycie` / `/g buylife`)

Guilds can recover lost lives using `/kupzycie` (aliases `/wykup`, `/buylife`), provided `buy-life.enabled` is active:
- **Payment type (`buy-life.payment-type`):** `ITEMS` (items), `MONEY` (Vault), or `BOTH` (items + Vault).
- **Cost scaling (`buy-life.scaling-cost`):** progressively increases the purchase cost for each life above `war.lives`.
- **Limits:** purchase cooldown (`buy-life.cooldown`) and maximum life limit (`buy-life.allow-buy-up-to-max-lives`).

### Anti-Farming Protection

To prevent abusing dummy guilds to farm lives and stats, the `war.anti-farming` section provides:
- Minimum member count for victim guilds (`min-victim-members`).
- Minimum age requirement for victim guilds (`min-victim-age`).
- Cooldown on gaining lives from the same guild (`victim-cooldown`).
- IP protection preventing farming against the same IP address (`protect-same-ip`).

Members of the same guild can't damage each other by default either — that is
controlled by `damage-guild`.

:::note[Other options]
`lives-repeating-symbol` sets the appearance of the `{G-LIVES-SYMBOL}`
placeholder — separate symbols for full and empty lives, and for any surplus
above `war-lives`.
:::

## Banning a guild

An admin can ban a guild with `/ga ban <tag> <time> <reason>`. The ban
expires after the given time; `/ga unban <tag>` also lifts it.
